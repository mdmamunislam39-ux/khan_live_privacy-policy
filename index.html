1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide and improve Khan Live:

a. Account & Profile Information

  • Registration Data: Email address, username, password (securely managed via Firebase Authentication), or Google account profile details if you sign in with Google.
  • Optional Profile Details: Display name, profile picture, cover photo, bio, gender, date of birth, country, phone number, and WhatsApp contact (if you voluntarily provide them).
  • Account Identifiers & Roles: Unique User ID, session ID, online/last-seen status, and role information (e.g., User, Host, Agency, BD, or Reseller).

b. User-Generated Content (UGC) & Live Interactions

  • Live Audio & Video Streams: Real-time audio and video transmitted when you broadcast or join a live room.
  • Chat & Social Activity: Public room chat messages, emojis, room titles/covers, follower/following lists, friend lists, blocked users, and gift interactions.
  • Uploaded Media: Images or files you choose to upload for your profile avatar, cover image, or room cover.

c. Virtual Currency & Transaction Records

  • Records of virtual coins, diamonds, virtual gifts sent/received, store item purchases, and host/agency commission or reward histories.

d. Device, Technical & Security Data

  • Device Identifiers: Device ID used strictly for account security, fraud prevention, and enforcing device-level bans against abusive users.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver notifications.
  • Network & App Integrity Data: Internet connectivity state and Firebase App Check attestation tokens to protect against bots, tampering, and unauthorized API access.
  • Local Storage: Session preferences and cached settings stored locally on your device.

2. Device Permissions We Request

Khan Live requests only the device permissions necessary for its core live-streaming and social features:

Permission Purpose
Camera Used to broadcast live video streams and apply real-time on-device beauty filters and AR stickers during video sessions.
Microphone & Audio Settings Used for voice communication in live audio/video rooms, hosting broadcasts, and controlling audio routing.
Bluetooth Used to connect wireless headphones, earbuds, and Bluetooth microphones during live streaming.
Foreground Service (Camera / Microphone / Media Playback) Keeps your live audio/video session active without interruption while you multitask or lock your screen.
Photos / Media / File Picker Used when you voluntarily choose a photo from your device to set as your profile picture, cover photo, or live room cover.
Notifications Used to alert you about live broadcasts from hosts you follow, gifts, account activity, and important service announcements.
Internet & Network State Required to connect to streaming servers, load content, and monitor connection stability.

You can manage or revoke any of these permissions at any time through your device's system settings. Please note that revoking certain permissions (such as Camera or Microphone) may limit live-streaming features.

3. Face Beauty & AR Effects (On-Device Processing)

No Biometric Data Collection: Khan Live includes optional real-time beauty filters, virtual makeup, face reshaping, and AR stickers powered by on-device GPU processing and MediaPipe Face Landmarker. All facial landmark detection occurs strictly locally on your device in real time. We do not extract, store, transmit, sell, or share facial geometry, faceprints, or biometric identifiers on our servers or with any third party. Temporary frame buffers are immediately discarded after each video frame is rendered.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share data with trusted infrastructure and service providers necessary to operate Khan Live:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, App Check) — for account login, database hosting, notifications, and app security. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In — for optional one-tap sign-in using your Google account.
  • LiveKit (WebRTC) & Dedicated VPS Infrastructure — for low-latency real-time audio/video streaming, backend APIs, PostgreSQL data storage, and media file hosting.
  • Stripe — for secure processing of in-app payment transactions where enabled. (Stripe Privacy Policy)
  • External Apps (e.g., WhatsApp) — if you tap a support or contact link that opens WhatsApp or a web browser, your interaction with that external service is governed by its respective privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the public.

6. Payments, Virtual Currency & Transactions

When you purchase virtual coins or other digital items, payments are processed securely through authorized payment processors (such as Stripe or Google Play Billing, where applicable). Khan Live does not collect or store your full credit/debit card numbers or banking credentials on our servers. We only retain transaction metadata (such as transaction ID, amount, timestamp, and virtual currency credited) for accounting, dispute resolution, and fraud prevention.

Because Khan Live is a live social platform, information you share publicly—such as your username, profile photo, bio, live audio/video streams, and public room chat messages—can be viewed by other users.

  • Moderation & Safety Tools: Users can block other users and report abusive behavior, inappropriate streams, or policy violations.
  • Enforcement: We reserve the right to review reported content, suspend or permanently ban offending accounts/devices, and remove content that violates our community standards.

8. Children's Privacy & Child Safety (CSAE Policy)

Strict 18+ Policy & Zero Tolerance for Child Exploitation:
Khan Live is intended strictly for users aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 (or under 13 in any jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data to Khan Live, please contact us immediately so we can delete the account and data.

Khan Live maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any account involved in grooming, exploiting, or sharing harmful content involving minors will be permanently banned without notice, and relevant details will be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and appropriate law enforcement authorities.

9. Data Security & Retention

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Khan Live app and our servers are encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS, and live WebRTC audio/video streams are encrypted using DTLS/SRTP.
  • Access Controls: Backend databases and storage are protected with authentication rules, role-based access controls, and Firebase App Check.
  • Retention: We retain your personal data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes, enforce bans, and comply with legal or financial record-keeping obligations.

10. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your Khan Live account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Via Email Request: Send an email to support@khanlive.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request — Khan Live.
  2. Include your registered Email Address and Username / User ID so we can verify ownership of the account.
  3. Once verified, we will process your deletion request within 7 to 30 days and send you a confirmation email.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted Data: Your login credentials, profile details (name, bio, photos, phone/WhatsApp), social connections (followers/friends), FCM tokens, and active session data are permanently deleted.
  • Temporarily Retained Data: Limited financial transaction records, commission/payout logs, or moderation/ban records may be retained only as required by applicable law, tax/accounting regulations, or fraud/abuse prevention.
